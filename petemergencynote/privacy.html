ItemPurposeStorage
Pet profile (name, species, birth date, weight, sex, chronic conditions, clinic contact, etc.)Adjust urgency assessment and build the vet summary cardLocal database on device (SwiftData)

We do not share, sell, or provide data to third parties (there is no server, so nothing is transmitted).

The lifetime unlock is handled via Apple In-App Purchase; payment is processed by Apple. We do not collect or store payment information.

Delete individual records/profiles in the app, or delete the app to remove all data from your device.

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
