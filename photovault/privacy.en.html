Privacy Policy — Photo Vault Pro (사진금고)

Last updated: 2026-06-21

Photo Vault Pro is designed with privacy first.

1. Data We Collect

Photo Vault Pro collects, transmits, and stores no personal data. Your photos, videos, location data, face-recognition data, and analysis results are processed only on your device and on the external storage (SSD) you choose.

2. Photos & Videos

Photo/video access is used solely for backup, organization, and restore features. All processing is on-device and never sent to any external server. AI classification, search, deduplication, and face recognition run entirely on-device using Apple's Vision/NaturalLanguage frameworks.

3. Network

Photo Vault Pro performs no network communication for photo/video processing. No ad SDKs, no analytics trackers, no third-party data transmission.

4. Payments

The lifetime Pro unlock is handled by the Apple App Store (StoreKit); payment information is managed by Apple. We do not collect payment data.

5. Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes in-app data (index, settings). Original photos copied to your external SSD remain under your control.
