FAQ

Q. How do I bind the Action Button?

iPhone Settings → Action Button → swipe to "Shortcut" → Choose Shortcut → search "pomu" → pick "Log a tiny win". Now pressing the side button records one habit, even from your pocket.

Q. Why doesn't the Dynamic Island show anything?

Live Activities must be enabled in Settings → Face ID & Passcode → "Live Activities" (also under Settings → pomu). Then trigger one habit log; the bubble appears for ~30 seconds and ends.

Q. How do I see my pet in StandBy?

Add the pomu widget to your home screen first (Edit Home Screen → + → pomu). Then charge your iPhone horizontally on a stand — StandBy will pick up the medium/large widget automatically.

Q. Where is my data?

Everything is on your device. There is no account, no server. With pomu Pro, your data can sync across devices through your private iCloud.

Q. How do I cancel pomu Pro?

iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → pomu → Cancel. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Q. Is this a habit tracker or a pet game?

Both, but really neither. pomu is meant to feel like your iPhone is a tiny living thing that quietly notices your tiny wins. There is no grinding, no failure, no streak you have to defend.
