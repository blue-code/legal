Privacy Policy

Effective: June 12, 2026 · App: No Silence (틈 없이)

Summary: we do not collect personal data. The game connects two nearby devices directly over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi (Multipeer Connectivity). It uses no server and no internet. Your nickname and answers travel only between the two devices and are never sent to us or stored anywhere.

What we do NOT collect

  • Name, contacts, email, or account (no login)
  • Photos, location, or contacts
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers

Local Network permission

Used only to discover and connect to the nearby device. No data leaves your devices during this process.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details; only transaction information for purchase validation is processed via Apple.

Children

Suitable for general audiences; no personal data is collected.

Third parties

No data is shared with third parties (none is collected).

隐私政策

生效日期: 2026年6月12日 · 应用: 不冷场 (No Silence)

摘要：我们不收集任何个人信息。 本游戏让附近的两台设备通过蓝牙和点对点 Wi-Fi 近距离连接（Multipeer Connectivity）直接通信。不使用服务器，也不使用互联网。昵称和答案仅在两台设备之间传递，绝不会发送给我们或存储于任何地方。

仅用于发现并连接附近的设备。此过程中没有任何数据离开您的设备。

应用内购买

购买由 Apple App Store 处理。我们绝不会获取您的支付信息；仅通过 Apple 处理用于校验购买的交易信息。

儿童

适合所有年龄，且不收集任何个人信息。

第三方
