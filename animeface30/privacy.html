English

In one line This app collects no personal data at all. It has no networking code, and the drawings you make and the photos you take never leave your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal information — no account, login, email address, name, location, advertising identifier, usage statistics or crash logs. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking technology of any kind.

2. Data kept on your device only

  • The drawings you make (stroke data and preview images)
  • Photos taken in paper mode
  • Course progress, streak, unlocks and app settings

All of this lives inside the app's own iOS container and is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it. There is no cloud sync. Settings → Export lets you create a backup file and save it to the Files app; that file is then yours to manage.

3. Camera and photos

The camera is used only in paper mode, to photograph a drawing you made on paper so the app can check its proportions. The photo is processed entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere. Photo library access is requested as add-only (to save finished drawings and videos). The app never reads or browses your photo library.

4. Purchases

Unlocking the full course is handled by Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not accessible to this app. The app only learns whether a purchase exists.

5. Children

The app is rated for ages 4 and up. Because it collects no personal data, it collects no data from children either.
