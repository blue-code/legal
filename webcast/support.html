English

What is WebCast? A browser that detects videos on web pages and sends them to your Chromecast or DLNA smart TV.

  • Casting doesn't start? Make sure your iPhone and the TV/Chromecast are on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • No video detected? The site must expose a directly playable video (MP4/HLS/DASH). DRM‑protected services (YouTube, Netflix, etc.) cannot be cast.
  • DLNA devices not found? DLNA discovery needs Local Network permission — allow it when prompted.
  • Purchases: The unlock is a one‑time, non‑consumable purchase. Use “Restore Purchase” on a new device.
