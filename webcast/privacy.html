WebCast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

English

WebCast is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No data collection. We have no servers and no analytics. We do not track you.
  • On‑device only. Your browsing history and bookmarks are stored locally on your device and never leave it.
  • Local network. The app uses your local network solely to discover and connect to Chromecast and DLNA devices for casting. This data is not transmitted to us.
  • Purchases. In‑app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.
  • Web browsing. Websites you visit are governed by their own privacy policies.

한국어

  • 데이터 미수집 — 서버도 분석 도구도 없으며 사용자를 추적하지 않습니다.
  • 기기 내 저장 — 방문 기록과 북마크는 오직 사용자 기기에만 저장됩니다.

  • 결제 — 인앱 구매는 Apple이 처리하며, 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다.
