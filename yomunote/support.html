Where are my recordings and transcripts stored?

Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded — YomuNote has no servers for your content and works fully in airplane mode.

Does transcription work without an internet connection?

Yes. Recording, transcription, speaker separation, summarization, search, and export all work offline. The only network use in the app is a one-time download of a speech-recognition or summary model file the first time you select a tier that isn't already on your device — this never includes any of your recordings or content.

What's the difference between Free, Pro, and Ultra?

Free is length-limited and uses the smallest on-device model. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the length limit and unlocks a more accurate model. Ultra is a subscription that unlocks the most accurate model, unlimited AI summaries, and semantic search across past meetings. See the in-app paywall for current pricing and details.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings → Restore Purchases.

My employer restricts cloud transcription apps — does YomuNote qualify?

YomuNote makes no network calls involving your recordings, transcripts, or summaries (see the Privacy Policy for the one disclosed exception: a one-time, content-free model-file download). If your policy also restricts moving content between apps, enable Shadow-IT Compliance Mode in Settings, which disables the share sheet and copy/paste for transcript and summary content.
