YomuNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026 · 日本語版は準備中です（English version is current）

YomuNote does not collect your recordings, transcripts, or summaries. Every meeting recording, transcript, and AI-generated summary is processed and stored entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers or any third party. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no analytics SDKs. YomuNote works fully in airplane mode. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

1. Information we collect

None. YomuNote operates no developer servers for your content. Your audio recordings, transcript text, speaker labels, and AI-generated summaries exist only inside your iPhone's app storage.

2. Information stored on your device only

  • Audio recordings and their on-device transcripts
  • Speaker labels produced by on-device diarization
  • AI-generated summaries and the templates you choose
  • App settings, including your selected speech-recognition model tier

All of it is stored using iOS's standard app-sandbox file protection.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to record meetings and voice memos and to transcribe them on your device. Recordings are never sent off your device.

YomuNote requests no other permissions in v1.0 — no location, no contacts, no photo library access.

4. Network use (the only exception)

YomuNote's core functionality — recording, transcription, diarization, summarization, search, export — works fully offline, including in airplane mode. There is exactly one network use in the entire app: on first use of a speech-recognition or summarization model tier that isn't bundled with the app, YomuNote downloads that model's files once. This is a one-directional download of a static software asset — comparable to a routine app update — and transmits nothing about you or your content. It is disclosed in-app before it happens. No other feature makes any network request.

5. Sharing & selling

We never share or sell your data, because we never receive it. The app contains no ad networks, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no third-party crash reporters that transmit information off your device.

6. Export

Exporting a transcript or summary (Markdown, plain text, SRT/VTT captions, or PDF) is entirely user-initiated. You decide where an exported file goes — Files, AirDrop, email, or your own cloud storage. We have no access to those files.

7. In-app purchases

YomuNote offers optional one-time and subscription in-app purchases, processed entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment information.

8. Children

YomuNote is a productivity tool for meeting transcription and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal data from any user, it carries an age rating of 4+.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will note it here and in the app's update notes.
