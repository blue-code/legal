Miru — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-07-19 · Last updated: 2026-07-19

Miru is an on-device AI companion. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: by default, nothing you say to Miru ever leaves your device, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Miru has no account system, no analytics, and no servers that we operate. Your conversations, your companion's settings, and any notes Miru remembers are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud/device backup, controlled entirely by you).

The on-device brain (default)

By default, Miru generates replies using Apple's on-device Foundation Models, which run locally on your iPhone using Apple silicon. Your messages are processed on the device and are not sent to us or to any third party.

Bring-your-own-key (optional)

Miru offers an optional setting to use your own third-party AI provider (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) by entering your own API key. If — and only if — you enable this, your messages are sent directly from your device to the provider you selected, using your key, under that provider's privacy policy and your account with them. Miru does not receive, proxy, log, or store these messages or your key on any server. Your API key is stored solely in your device's Keychain and is transmitted only to the provider you chose. You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Voice

Text-to-speech (Miru's spoken voice) uses Apple's on-device speech synthesis. No audio is sent to us.

Purchases

The optional lifetime "Miru Pro" unlock is processed by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We never see your payment information; entitlement is verified on your device.

Children

Miru is intended for users aged 17 and older, because open AI conversation may touch on mature topics. It is not directed to children.

A note on AI

Miru is an AI companion. It can be inaccurate and is not a substitute for professional advice, therapy, or crisis support. If you are in distress, please reach out to a qualified professional or a local crisis line.
