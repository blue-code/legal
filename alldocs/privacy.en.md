# Privacy Policy — AllDocs

**Effective:** June 22, 2026 · **Last updated:** June 22, 2026

**Summary:** AllDocs runs no servers. The documents you open and the edits you make are processed **entirely on your device**; the developer (blue-code) cannot collect or access them. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no tracking, and no App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. The App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected."**

---

## 1. Information We Collect / Store

AllDocs sends no information to any developer server. No such server exists.

- **Document content** — txt, md, doc, docx, hwp, and hwpx files you open are viewed, parsed, and rendered **only on your device**. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Edits** — Editing and saving txt/md writes only back to the original file location you chose (Files app / device / your iCloud Drive, i.e. your own storage).
- **App settings & recents** — Theme, text size, references to recently opened documents (security-scoped bookmarks), and display preferences are stored only in the app's area on your device.

## 2. Data Sharing

**AllDocs shares no data with third parties.**

- No analytics tools
- No crash-reporting SDKs
- No advertising or tracking identifiers (no IDFA)
- No App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt
- No developer backend server

## 3. Permissions & File Access

AllDocs accesses only the documents you **explicitly select or share from another app** (via iOS security-scoped access). It does not scan your storage; it reads only the files you open. It requests no camera, contacts, or location permissions.

## 4. Payments (In-App Purchase)

AllDocs Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase) is handled through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes the payment; the developer has no access to your payment details and only checks your entitlement status on-device.

## 5. Children

AllDocs is not directed at children and collects no personal information.
