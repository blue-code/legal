FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Past purchases will sync automatically when signed into the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?

In-app purchase refunds are handled directly by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are these real stock charts?

No. All chart data is synthetic, created for learning purposes. No live market data is provided.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — all features work without an internet connection.
