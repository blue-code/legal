Stage2 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026 · App: Stage2 (com.blueCode.Stage2) · Developer: blue-code

Stage2 does not collect any personal data. The app is a fully on-device educational tool for growth-stock trading discipline.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Stage2 has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — account settings, watchlist, trade journal, and learning progress — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). This data never leaves your device through us. It is included in your own encrypted device/iCloud backups if you have those enabled, which are controlled entirely by you and Apple.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Pro" purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network use

Stage2 works fully offline. The only network connection is to Apple's App Store for processing and restoring the in-app purchase. The app does not fetch market data or contact any third-party service.

Children

Stage2 is rated 4+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Not investment advice

Stage2 is an educational tool. It does not provide investment advice, recommendations, or live market data, and it is not affiliated with any author or publisher.
