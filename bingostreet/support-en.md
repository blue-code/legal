# Support — Bingo Street (빙고 스트릿)

**Last updated: May 26, 2026**

Bingo Street is an iOS game where you fill a 5×5 bingo board by finding everyday street sights and verifying them with photos you take yourself. If you need help, check the FAQ below first, and email us if your question isn't answered.

## Contact

Bug reports, feature requests, and questions:
[dev@bingostreet.app](mailto:dev@bingostreet.app)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Do I need an account?**
No. Bingo Street starts instantly — no sign-up or login required.

**Q. Can I fill a cell with a photo from my gallery?**
No. For authenticity, only photos captured live inside the app count as verification. The app does not even request gallery permission.

**Q. Do I need the internet to play with friends?**
No. Multiplayer works only over local communication (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi). Nearby devices connect directly to each other; no internet server is used.

**Q. I denied camera permission. What now?**
Camera permission is required for photo verification. Enable it in Settings → Bingo Street → Camera.

**Q. Are my photos uploaded to a server?**
No. Photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. Even in multiplayer, original photos are not transmitted to other devices. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html) for details.

**Q. Is location recorded in my photos?**
Location may be embedded in a photo's metadata when you take it. It is used only to display where the photo was taken on the history screen and is never sent to a server.

**Q. How do I delete my records?**
You can delete individual game records from the history detail screen. Deleting the app removes all local data.

**Q. What devices are supported?**
iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later (portrait orientation). Korean and English are supported.
