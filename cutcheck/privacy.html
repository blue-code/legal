CutCheck Privacy Policy

CutCheck does not collect your data. Selected CSV matrices and media are read locally on your Mac. No media, filenames, matrices or reports are uploaded by the app. There is no advertising, analytics or developer-operated account service.

Client presets and your interface language are stored locally in the app’s container. Reports are saved only to a location you choose. Remove presets in Settings & Pro using Delete Preset. Removing the app’s local container deletes its stored preferences and presets; it does not delete your source media or exported reports.

Apple handles the optional one-time Pro purchase and restoration. CutCheck reads verified StoreKit entitlements locally to enable batch checks and client-name-branded reports. It does not operate a purchase server or receive your payment card information.

Privacy, support and feedback links open your browser or mail application. If you voluntarily contact us, we use the information you provide to respond. Do not send confidential client media. Apple’s App Store and your email provider apply their own privacy policies.

CutCheck Datenschutz

CutCheck erfasst keine Nutzerdaten. Ausgewählte CSV-Matrizen und Medien werden lokal auf Ihrem Mac gelesen. Die App lädt keine Medien, Dateinamen, Matrizen oder Berichte hoch. Keine Werbung, Nutzungsanalyse oder vom Entwickler betriebenen Konten.
