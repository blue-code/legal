Terms of Use — Fineprint

Last updated: 6 August 2026

WHAT THIS APP IS

Fineprint reads a residential lease and shows you what it says: the terms that matter, quoted

from your own document, and the deadlines those terms create.

WHAT THIS APP IS NOT

It is not a lawyer and it is not legal advice. It does not tell you whether a clause holds up,

whether to sign, or what anything might be worth. It has no opinion about your lease.

Nothing it shows you is a determination about the law. Where it compares a term against a

statute, it shows you the sentence in your lease, the rule it was compared against, and a

question you can ask — and that comparison only runs where a licensed attorney has reviewed and

approved it. Where none has, the app tells you no checks ran.

A term this app does not flag has not been cleared. It reads a defined list, not your whole

lease, and it says so on every report.

ACCURACY

The app is deterministic: it looks for specific clauses using specific patterns, and it refuses

rather than guesses when it cannot read something. It will miss things. It reports what it could

not read, on every report, because a report that hides its own gaps is worse than no report.

Do not rely on this app alone for a decision that matters. If something in your lease worries

you, talk to a tenant attorney or your local legal aid organisation.

PURCHASES

Single-lease purchases are consumable: they unlock one report, permanently, on this device. They

cannot be restored by Apple, and this app has no server to back them up. If you delete the app,

your reports and any unused credits are gone.

"Every lease" is a one-time purchase and can be restored through the App Store in the usual way.

There is no subscription in this app.
