Privacy Policy — Fineprint

Last updated: 6 August 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Fineprint has no networking code in it. It cannot send your lease anywhere, because it has no

way to send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no

advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs.

You can verify this yourself: turn on Airplane Mode and use the app normally. Everything works.

WHAT THE APP HANDLES

Your lease. When you open a PDF, the app reads it on this device. It keeps the extracted text of

the document — not the original file — so that a future rule pack can re-analyse the same lease

without asking you for it again, and so that re-opening a lease you already paid for does not

charge you twice.

Your reports. Stored on this iPhone, in the app's own storage. They are included in an encrypted

iPhone backup if you make one; that backup is between you and Apple, and we never see it.

WHAT LEAVES THE DEVICE

Nothing that we send. Two things happen outside the app, and both are Apple's:

  • Purchases. When you buy something, Apple processes the payment. We receive no payment details

and no identity — only a transaction identifier that the app records locally so your report

stays unlocked. See Apple's privacy policy for what Apple does with a purchase.

  • Crash reports, if you have turned them on in iOS Settings. Those go to Apple, and we can see

aggregate crash traces in App Store Connect. They contain no document text.

If you tap a link that opens Safari, you have left the app and the ordinary rules of the web

apply.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. There is no server to collect it with.

DELETION

Deleting the app deletes your reports and any unused purchase credits. There is no backup we can

restore them from, and no account to recover. That is the direct cost of the app never sending

your lease anywhere, and it is stated at purchase for exactly that reason.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated text ships inside the app with the version that changes it.

There is no remote configuration and nothing here can change without an App Store update.
