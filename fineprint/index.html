Fineprint: Read Your Lease — Legal & Support

Fineprint reads a residential lease on your iPhone and shows you what it says: the terms that matter, quoted from your own document. It has no networking code, no account, and no analytics.

Nothing leaves your phone. The app cannot send your lease anywhere, because it has no way to send anything anywhere. Turn on Airplane Mode and use it normally — everything works.

Questions people ask first

  • Does it need the internet? No. There is no networking code in the app at all. It works in Airplane Mode.
  • Where do my reports go? Nowhere. They stay in the app's storage on that iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, and there is no cloud backup to restore from — that is the trade for your lease never leaving the device.
  • Can I restore a purchase? “Every lease” is a one-time purchase and restores through the App Store normally. Single-lease purchases are consumable, and Apple cannot restore those; this is stated on the purchase screen before payment.
  • Does it tell me if my lease is legal? No, and it never will. It shows you what your lease says and, where a licensed attorney has approved a rule for your area, the question to ask about a clause. It does not decide anything about the law, and not flagging something is never a clearance.
  • Does it work on a scan? Yes, by recognising the text in the picture — which is less certain than reading an e-signed PDF. The report tells you which pages were read that way.
