What is Komorebi?

Komorebi (木漏れ日) is Japanese for sunlight filtering through leaves — fleeting, gentle, irreplaceable. The app is built for people who want to slow down and notice. No artificial sharpening, no AI smoothing — just light, shadow, and the soft texture of analog film.

Features

  • Five film looksRaw · K-Portra 400 · T-Max 400 · Ektar 100 · F-Chrome 100, with adjustable intensity.
  • Always-silent shutterNo sound, ever — capture quiet moments without disturbing them.
  • Anti-AI modePreserves natural grain and skin texture instead of over-smoothing.
  • Exposure controlA simple slider for brighter or darker shots.
  • Self-timer & interval3s / 10s timer and interval (time-lapse) capture with an optional shot limit.
  • Landscape & portraitHold your phone sideways for wide photos.
  • Action Button & ShortcutsOpen the camera or cycle filters instantly.
  • Private by designPhotos are saved only to your library. Nothing leaves your device.
