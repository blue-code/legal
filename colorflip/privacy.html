English

Summary: Colorflip Arrows collects no personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads or analytics, and the game works offline. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. There is no sign-up or login, and nothing — name, email, location or device identifiers — is sent to us. We do not operate a server.

2. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ad SDKs, analytics SDKs or crash reporters, and it does not track you.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Best stars per puzzle, the last puzzle played, and which tutorials you have seen
  • Settings (sound, haptics, pull colored arrows instantly)
  • A cached copy of your purchase state (the Apple App Store is the source of truth)

This data lives in a file inside the app and is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. In-app purchase

Unlocking the remaining 50 puzzles is a one-time purchase (not a subscription) handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive payment details. Purchase verification and restore also go through Apple (Apple Privacy Policy).

5. Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Contact: digimaru@gmail.com
