Nowbox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

English

Nowbox does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. The app makes no network requests of its own.

  • What you type stays on your device. Task titles, time blocks, reviews, statistics and settings are stored only in the app's local database on your iPhone.
  • Nothing is transmitted. We cannot see your data, because it never leaves your device. There is no backup or sync in this version.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally by iOS. They are not push notifications and involve no server.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Optional feedback. If you tap "Send feedback" in Settings, the app opens a Google Form in your browser, pre-filled with the app name, app version and interface language. Anything you write there is sent by you, to us, only when you submit that form. The app itself sends nothing.
  • Deleting your data — Settings › Delete everything, or delete the app. Either removes all of it permanently.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to request access to, correct, or export from us. You can export your own data to a file at any time from Settings.

Children: the app is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
