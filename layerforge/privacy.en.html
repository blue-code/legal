Privacy Policy — Layerforge

Effective: October 4, 2026 · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Summary: Layerforge is a layer-based image editor for Windows and runs no servers of its own. The images and projects you open, AI processing and app settings are all handled and stored only on your PC. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no user data is sent to blue-code.

1. Information the app handles

  • Images and project files — Files you open or import (JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, SVG, PSD/PSB, camera RAW, Layerforge .comp projects) are read and written only on your PC for editing. They are never uploaded.
  • App settings — Preferences such as panel sizes, tool options and the recent files list are stored in the app settings file on this device.
  • Pro status hint — To show the right state quickly at startup, the last known Layerforge Pro status (yes/no) is saved in the same settings file.

2. AI features (Object Selection, Select Subject, Remove Background)

AI features run directly on your PC using models included in the app (U²-Net, EfficientSAM). Your images are never sent to a server, and the app does not download models or collect results.

3. In-app purchase (Layerforge Pro)

Layerforge Pro is an add-on sold through the Microsoft Store. Payment and order processing are handled by Microsoft, and the Microsoft Privacy Statement applies to the information processed for the purchase. Layerforge uses the Microsoft Store features built into Windows only to check whether your Layerforge Pro license is active and to show the price listed in the Store. Layerforge does not receive or store your name, email address, payment details or other account information.

4. Sharing with third parties

Layerforge does not provide or share any user data with third parties.

  • No analytics tools
  • No crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers
  • No servers of our own

5. Device features used

  • File access — Only the files and folders you choose through file dialogs, drag and drop or "Open with" are read and written.
  • Folder change detection — While a .comp project is open, only that folder is watched so that changes made by other programs appear on screen.
  • Clipboard — Images are read or written only when you run Copy or Paste.
  • Graphics device — The GPU is used for display and AI processing.

6. Retention and deletion

App settings are stored only on this device and are removed when you uninstall Layerforge. Images and project files you create stay wherever you saved them and remain under your control.

7. Accounts

Layerforge does not require registration or sign-in.

8. Children's privacy

Layerforge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and their effective date will be posted on this page.
