FAQ

  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings → Restore Purchase. The app verifies your purchase through Apple's App Store — no account or login needed.
  • Does the app need an internet connection? No. Moonwhisper Tarot runs fully offline; readings, journal entries, and settings all stay on your device.
  • Does my data sync across devices? Not currently — data is stored locally only, and is removed if you delete the app.
